Why we are asking

A dream of five years, standing half-built

For over five years, the well-wishers of the Madanapalle Pyramid Spiritual Society have dreamed of building a meditation pyramid, a calm, open space where anyone, of any background, can sit, breathe, and find peace. Pyramid meditation has helped thousands across our region; we wanted to bring that gift home to Madanapalle.

In August 2024 we formed a team and began searching for land. We approached government officials for a donation of land, but no help came. When every door seemed closed, one family opened theirs.

My parents, Sri J. Venkatarayappa Chetty and Smt. J. Anuradha, meditators for nearly 25 years, together with Smt. J. Swarnalatha, donated 11,763 square feet of their own land, worth about ₹1 crore, so that the pyramid could finally begin. The registration was completed on 5 November 2024.

Since then, spiritual well-wishers in and around Madanapalle have given from their hearts, around ₹18,00,000 in all. Every rupee has gone into the ground: the pooja, the foundation, the borewell, the electricity, the culvert, and the pillars you will see below. That money is now finished, and construction has stopped. This is our appeal to the wider world to help us carry it forward.
